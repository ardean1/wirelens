Unwanted connections?

WireLens shows which programs on this PC are talking to which hosts on the network. A “flag” means something looked unusual by a simple rule (unknown hostname, odd port, burst of short connections, or your optional blocklist) — not that it is malware, and not antivirus.

  1. Note the process name and the remote host or IP.
  2. Ask if that app is expected to be online (browser, updater, sync — often yes).
  3. If you do not recognize it, investigate calmly — WireLens does not remove software.
